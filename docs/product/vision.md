# Product Vision

## The idea
Creature World is a tiny living ecosystem in the browser. Soft, furry, octopus-like creatures
drift, rest, and play in their pool. They notice you — your cursor is a curious presence in their world —
and each reacts in its own way. Over time they interact with each other and, eventually, communicate
with the human watching them.

It should feel like **discovering** something alive, not using a website.

## Experience principles
1. **Alive before interactive.** Even if you do nothing, the creatures breathe, blink, and wander.
2. **Noticed, not controlled.** The visitor is an observer the creatures react to, not a player issuing commands.
3. **Personality over spectacle.** Small, believable behaviors beat flashy effects.
4. **Gentle and cozy.** Soft shapes, calm motion, no fail states, no UI clutter.
5. **Smooth always.** Stuttering kills the illusion of life; performance is a feature.

## Long-term pillars
| Pillar | Description | When |
|---|---|---|
| Presence | Creatures perceive and react to the cursor | MVP |
| Personality | Traits make each creature distinct | MVP |
| Society | Creatures greet, play, follow, and avoid each other | Post-MVP |
| Communication | Expressive sounds → LLM-driven speech and conversation | Post-MVP |
| Memory | Creatures remember the visitor across visits | Post-MVP |

## Audience
- Portfolio visitors (recruiters, developers) who should be delighted within seconds.
- The owner, as a vehicle for learning modern front-end, simulation, and AI integration.
